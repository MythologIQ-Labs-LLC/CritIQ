# Code of Conduct

CritIQ is a technical open-source project. Participation should improve the product, its documentation, its evidence, or the quality of the technical discussion.

## Expected conduct

Contributors should:

- critique ideas, code, evidence, and decisions rather than attacking people;
- distinguish verified behavior from assumptions and preferences;
- provide reproducible evidence when making technical claims where practical;
- report security-sensitive findings through `SECURITY.md`;
- avoid discriminatory, threatening, harassing, or deliberately humiliating behavior;
- avoid flooding issues or pull requests with repetitive, automated, or low-signal content;
- respect maintainer requests to narrow scope or move unrelated work elsewhere;
- preserve useful technical history when correcting mistakes.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreement is normal. Useful disagreement looks like:

```text
claim
-> evidence or reproduction
-> affected behavior or contract
-> consequence
-> proposed correction
```

Confidence, repetition, or job title is not a substitute for evidence.

## Automated participation

AI-assisted and automated contribution methods are permitted under `GOVERNANCE.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

Automation does not excuse spam, unbounded activity, low-signal issue generation, or actions outside the authority delegated by a responsible human.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when behavior materially degrades safety, collaboration, or the technical record.

Enforcement should be proportional and should preserve useful technical evidence when practical.

## Scope

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-controlled collaboration spaces.
